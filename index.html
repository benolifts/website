GUIDE TO LIFTS IN THE UK

Lifts are a part of the UKs heritage that is ignored and forgotten. People often want to preserve old buildings but not the lifts (a car from the 1930s would not just be scrapped, but lifts from the 1930s get replaced without considerition). Building owners often just replace/modernize lifts thinking a new lift is better but don't think of the heritage thay are replacing.

STREET SCENES AND STREET FURNITURE

This section is about street scenes, and places which have not changed since they were built. There are many places that local councils want you to beleve that they are historic, when actually they are not as original as you are led to belive. What I am looking for is streets where the little details are original e.g. Lampposts, Traffic Lights, etc. which are disappearing fast as councils don't care about REAL historic items (they're only interested in supposedly "historic" sites which attract tourists). This section is all about scenes, and covers topic from building architecture to the history of lampposts and traffic lights.
